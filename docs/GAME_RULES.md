# നേരംപോക്ക് (NERAMPOKKU) — OFFICIAL GAME RULES & MECHANICS

> **Official Name:** നേരംപോക്ക്  
> **Tagline:** കളിക്കാം... സമയം പോക്കാം!  
> **Descriptor:** GAME BOT  

## 1. UNO Game Rules

### Deck Composition (108 Cards)
- **Number Cards (76 cards):** 4 Colors (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow).
  - One '0' per color (4 cards).
  - Two each of '1' through '9' per color (72 cards).
- **Action Cards (24 cards):**
  - **Skip (8 cards):** Next player is skipped.
  - **Reverse (8 cards):** In 3–6 player matches, flips direction (clockwise ↔ counter-clockwise). In 2-player matches, acts as a Skip.
  - **Draw Two (8 cards):** Next player draws 2 cards and forfeits their turn (unless +2 stacking is enabled).
- **Wild Cards (8 cards):**
  - **Wild (4 cards):** Player declares the active color.
  - **Wild Draw Four (4 cards):** Player declares color and next player must draw 4 cards and skip, OR Challenge.

### Legality & Matching
A card is legal to play if:
1. Its color matches the discard pile's active color.
2. Its value/symbol matches the discard pile's top card.
3. It is a Wild or Wild Draw Four card.

### Wild Draw Four Challenge
- Legality rule: A Wild Draw Four can legally only be played if the player holds **no** card matching the active color.
- If challenged:
  - If offender was illegal (held matching color): Offender draws 4 cards.
  - If offender was legal: Challenger draws 6 cards (4 + 2 penalty) and is skipped!

### "UNO!" Declaration & Penalty
- When playing a card that leaves a player with exactly 1 card, they must declare **"UNO!"**.
- If they fail to declare UNO, opponents have a 3-second grace window to tap **"CATCH UNO!"**.
- A caught player draws **2 penalty cards**.

## 2. LUDO Game Rules

### 4-Player Board Layout
- 15x15 grid with 4 color bases: Red (0), Green (13), Yellow (26), Blue (39).
- 52-square common outer perimeter track.
- 8 Safe Squares (4 start squares + 4 star squares).
- 5-step colored home stretch + central finish center. Total 56 steps to win.

### 6-Player Hexagonal Board Layout
- Regular hexagonal symmetry with 6 color bases: Red (0), Green (12), Yellow (24), Blue (36), Orange (48), Purple (60).
- 72-square common perimeter track.
- 12 Safe Squares (6 start squares + 6 star squares).
- 5-step colored home runway + central star finish. Total 76 steps to win.

### Gameplay Rules
1. **Exiting Base:** A dice roll of **6** is required to move a token from Base to the Start Square.
2. **Safe Squares:** Tokens on Start squares and Star squares cannot be captured. Multiple tokens may safely share safe squares.
3. **Capturing:** Landing on an opponent token on an unsafe square sends the opponent token back to Base (`step = -1`) and awards a **Bonus Dice Roll**.
4. **Consecutive Sixes Rule:** Rolling a 6 grants a bonus roll. Rolling three 6s in a row invalidates the third roll and forfeits the turn immediately.
5. **Exact Roll to Finish:** A token requires an exact dice roll to enter the Home Center. Reaching Home with a token awards a **Bonus Dice Roll**.
